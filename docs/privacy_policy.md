# Privacy Policy

**App:** Math Kingdom  
**Developer:** Forebear Productions  
**Effective date:** 20 August 2026  
**Last updated:** 20 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Math Kingdom (“the App”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you use the App.

Math Kingdom is a **children’s educational app**. We designed the App to store most learning data **locally on the device**. The App also displays **advertisements** through Google AdMob.

If you are a parent or guardian, please read this policy carefully.

---

## 1. Summary

| Topic | What we do |
|--------|------------|
| Account required | No |
| Data stored on our servers | No (current version) |
| Learning progress | Stored locally on the device |
| Advertising | Yes — Google AdMob (banner and full-screen ads) |
| Ads for children | Configured as **child-directed**; we request child-appropriate ad treatment |
| Analytics / crash reporting | Not enabled in the current version |
| Selling personal information | We do not sell personal information |

---

## 2. Information Stored Locally on the Device

The App may store the following information **only on the user’s device** using local storage:

- **Child profile information** — profile name and avatar selection  
- **Learning progress** — stars, streaks, module completion, rewards, and kingdom progress  
- **Parental controls** — a **hashed** parent PIN (not stored in plain text)  
- **App settings** — language preference, audio settings, and onboarding completion state  
- **Temporary app files** — files needed for audio playback or other on-device functions  

The App **does not** require account creation and **does not** ask children to submit:

- email addresses  
- phone numbers  
- precise location  
- contacts  
- photos, camera recordings, or microphone recordings  

---

## 3. Information Collected by Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays ads provided by **Google AdMob**. When ads are shown, **Google** may collect or receive certain information from the device, such as:

- **Advertising identifier** (where available and permitted on the device)  
- **Device information** — device type, operating system version, language, and general region  
- **Ad interaction data** — such as ad impressions and clicks  
- **IP address** (which may be used to infer approximate location)  

We configure the App to treat the experience as **child-directed** and request settings intended for apps used by children, including:

- child-directed treatment  
- under-age-of-consent treatment  
- a general-audience (**G**) maximum ad content rating  

Even with these settings, **Google**, as the ad provider, processes some device and ad-related data according to [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google Advertising Policies](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543).

**We do not receive your child’s name, learning answers, or profile content from AdMob.**

For more information about how Google uses data in advertising, visit:  
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

---

## 4. How We Use Information

We use locally stored information to:

- save a child’s learning progress and unlocked content  
- remember app settings and onboarding state  
- support parental access controls  
- provide the App’s educational features and game-like experience  

Advertising is used to **support the free version of the App**.

---

## 5. Data Sharing

### 5.1 Information we share

- **We do not sell** personal information.  
- **We do not operate our own backend servers** in the current version to collect child profile or learning data.  
- **Advertising data** may be processed by **Google LLC (AdMob)** when ads are displayed, as described in Section 3.

### 5.2 Information we do not share from our own storage

Based on the current App implementation, profile names, learning progress, and parent PIN hashes stored locally on the device are **not transmitted to Forebear Productions’ servers**.

## 6. Children’s Privacy

Math Kingdom is designed for young learners. We aim to limit data collection to what is needed for the App to function and to support a free, ad-supported experience.

- We do **not** knowingly collect online contact information from children through the App.  
- We do **not** require children to create accounts.  
- We configure ad requests for **child-directed** treatment.  
- Parents can use **parental controls** (parent PIN) to restrict access to certain areas such as settings and the parent dashboard.

Parents and guardians may contact us with questions or requests regarding their child’s use of the App (see Section 11).

This App is published on app stores that require privacy disclosures for children’s apps. For general information about children’s online privacy in the United States, see the FTC’s [COPPA FAQ](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-coppa-frequently-asked-questions).

## 9. Storage, Retention, and Deletion

- Local app data remains on the device until you **clear app storage**, **reset progress in the App**, or **uninstall** the App.  
- Temporary files created during normal App use may be removed automatically by the operating system or when the App no longer needs them.  
- We do not retain copies of locally stored child progress on our own servers in the current version.

**To delete local data:** uninstall the App or clear its storage in your device settings.

---

## 10. Security

We use reasonable measures within the App’s local architecture to protect stored information. However, no method of electronic storage is completely secure.

The parent PIN is stored in **hashed form** on the device. This helps avoid storing the plain PIN directly, but it should not be considered bank-grade security.
